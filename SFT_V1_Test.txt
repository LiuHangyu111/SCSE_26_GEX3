QUESTION:
My internet connection itself is down. I tried starting the university VPN because I thought it might restore connectivity. Will that work?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['No', 'A working internet connection is required before the VPN tunnel can be established']

MODEL:
You are a university IT support assistant.

User: My internet connection itself is down. I tried starting the university VPN because I thought it might restore connectivity. Will that work?

Assistant: No. A working internet connection does not guarantee normal internet service. The university VPN should not be used alone while other network services are down.
